Telegram Temporarily Banned in India

The Indian government has temporarily blocked Telegram across the country. The decision was taken to stop organized groups involved in spreading fake examination papers and online scams. Combatting Exam Fraud: The National Testing Agency (NTA) requested the block to halt organized cheating syndicates that were using public channels to defraud medical aspirants ahead of the NEET-UG 2026 re-examination on June 21.Financial Scams: Fraudsters were using anonymous Telegram channels to demand large sums of money from students and parents by falsely claiming they had early access to the upcoming exam papers.Manipulation of Evidence: Scammers were misusing Telegram's message-editing feature. They would swap new files or PDF question papers into old, existing posts after an exam had already taken place, fraudulently claiming they had leaked the exam content.
