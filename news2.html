Breaking News

The one-week restriction is aimed at disrupting organized cheating rackets using the platform ahead of the NEET-UG 2026 re-examination scheduled for June 21. The National Testing Agency (NTA) ordered a full nationwide re-test after the original May 3 examination was cancelled due to serious question paper leak allegations. The Telegram ban is part of a broader crackdown on online platforms to prevent the spread of fake exam papers and scams targeting students and parents. Fraudsters were reportedly using anonymous Telegram channels to demand large sums of money by falsely claiming they had early access to the upcoming exam papers. Additionally, scammers were misusing Telegram's message-editing feature to swap new files or PDF question papers into old, existing posts after an exam had already taken place, fraudulently claiming they had leaked the exam content.
